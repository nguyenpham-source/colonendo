Patient Hang Bui Age 68 Doctor Melvin Simien, MD Where Baylor Surgicare at Fort Worth
The short version

Good news overall. No cancer was found.

  • Good
    The exam was complete and high quality. The scope reached the very end of the colon, the bowel prep was good, and there were no complications.
  • Good
    No cancer anywhere.
  • Removed
    Two tiny polyps were the "precancerous" type (called sessile serrated lesions). They showed no abnormal cells and were removed completely during the exam.
  • Harmless
    The third polyp was just a harmless flap of lining (a "mucosal tag"), not a true polyp.
  • Common
    Small pouches in the colon wall (diverticulosis). Very common after age 60, usually causes no symptoms.
  • Plan
    Next colonoscopy in 5 years, around July 2031.
